# Geometry Lab — topology names and classification

The **Geometry Lab** is the container the review page uses for its mathematical view topologies.
It is a container, not a family: the lab holds mathematical topologies of different families —
iterated function systems, a complex dynamical system, and a combinatorial topology — and no
single label such as «IFS» or «Fractal» groups them. **Natural** is the classic disposition of
the primary hierarchy and is not part of that classification.

The selector shows exactly the short English names; the full names and the classification live in
the tooltips, the legend and this document, as explanation only.

## The four topologies and their families

The classification sentences are the same sentences the tooltips and the legend carry:

- Canopy — fractal tree generated through an Iterated Function System (IFS).
- Julia — complex dynamical system (not classified primarily as an IFS, even if it may use inverse iteration).
- Sierpiński — affine Iterated Function System (2D: gasket triangolare; 3D: polvere di tetraedro).
- Llull — combinatorial topology governed by the verified Llullian nine-domain rule; not a fractal and not an IFS.

## What each surface shows

- **Selector** (`Topologia` in the toolbar and the fullscreen bar): the short names only —
  Canopy, Julia, Sierpiński, Llull. The group itself states that the lab holds topologies of
  different families.
- **Tooltips**: the full name with its classification sentence, as listed above.
- **Legend** (the details panel «Legenda» and the fullscreen legend): whenever one of the four
  topologies governs the view, the legend repeats the different-families line and the four
  classification sentences.
- **Documentation**: this file.

The extended technical names («triangular gasket», «external coordinates», «Llullian
combinatorics» and similar) remain available only as explanation in these surfaces, never as
selector labels, and the lab never presents Canopy, Julia, Sierpiński and Llull as members of a
single «IFS» or «Fractal» category.
